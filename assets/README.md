# Assets

What ships inside OmniToaster, where it came from, and what it is for.
`docs/specs/toast.md` §8.2 is the specification; this file is the record of the
files themselves.

## `icon/`

The app icon in every size the exe and the tray need. `omnitoaster-512.png` is
the master; the rest are generated by `scripts/make-icons.ps1` (ADR-013).

## `builtin/pictures/` — 19 pictures, embedded in the exe

256 × 256 WebP, about 17 KB each (326 KB in all), **embedded** by
`crates/ot-store/src/builtin.rs` and named in the app by the key
`@builtin/<name>`. They are the default pictures for the categories below; a
template that names none of its own gets the one for its category. Phases 4, 5
and 6 bind them (the reminder types, the prayers, the weather).

**No picture yet** for Syuruk (sunrise) and Jumaat, which share `masjid-subuh`
and `masjid-zohor` until someone makes one; for snow, which Malaysia does not
need; and for the reminder types Task, Anniversary, Fatigue, Payment and Custom,
which show the app icon.

**To change or add one:** drop the original in, then

```bash
magick original.png -resize 256x256 -strip -quality 85 -define webp:method=6 -define webp:alpha-quality=90 builtin/pictures/<name>.webp
```

and add a line to `PICTURES` in `builtin.rs`, a label in both `.ftl` files
(`settings-builtin-<name>`), and a row above. `a_builtin_picture_is_small_and_decodes`
fails if a picture is not a WebP, is bigger than 256 px, or takes the total past
600 KB.

## `sound/` — 3 clips, **not yet embedded**

MP3, 128 kbps stereo, 44.1 kHz. Nothing plays them yet: the audio engine is
`ot-media`, which arrives with the phases that need it, and the clips are
embedded then, not before — five megabytes of dead weight in the exe until
something can play them would help nobody.

| File | For | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `alarm.mp3` | Alarm reminders | about 30 s |
| `azan.mp3` | the Azan, every prayer but Subuh | about 2 min |
| `azan-subuh.mp3` | the Azan for Subuh (Fajr) | about 3 min |

**Still to find: the default chime.** `notification.mp3` was supplied too, but
its tags say it is the *Button* sound from **HTC Sense 6** (© HTC, 2014): a
phone maker's own sound, which does not belong in a public MIT repository. It
is kept out of git. The default chime for reminders and weather — and the chime
that stands in for speech when no voice is installed
(`docs/specs/announcements.md`) — needs a sound whose licence is known.

## Provenance and licence

**All of it was supplied by the owner on 2026-11-15, and none of the licences
has been established.** What is known:

- The pictures were made by the owner (512 × 512 PNG; the mosque, weather,
  alarm-clock, meeting and training sets). Four more — `car_insurances`,
  `credit_card`, `subscription_bill`, `games` — show real brand marks (an
  insurer, banks and Visa, a streaming service, a game's interface). They are
  **not** in this repository, on the owner's decision, and wait for a rights
  check; they would be the Payment picture and its kin.
- The audio carries ID3 titles `A01_Alarm01`, `A02_Azan` and `A01_Azan_Subuh`,
  numbering that looks like a sound pack. The source and its terms are not
  recorded anywhere.

The owner chose to commit the three clips knowing this. **Phase 9's terms check
(`PROPOSAL.md` §13) has to settle all of it before a release is promoted as
redistributable**; until then this is a known gap, not a cleared one. Anything
that cannot be cleared is replaced, and the file name stays.
